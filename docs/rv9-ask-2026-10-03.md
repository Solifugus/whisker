# One module staged, 2026-10-03

Short, and there is no defect in it this time.

## `modules/wstat/` — new, uncommitted in your tree

Die temperature, available heap and uptime on the panel, kept up to date.

```
wstat > /w0           until stopped
wstat 120 > /w0       for 120 seconds
```

**Why it exists.** whisker asked Matthew what the C5 panel is *for*, because
`hit` assumes a pointing device and the C5 has none — so either §2.6's second
row (a focus traversal, `activate`, a focus mark) was one more iteration, or it
was never. The answer was the hardware: *"The C5 panel is write only and has no
controls on it. I interact via serial console or over ssh."* So: never. The
console commands, the panel shows, and a **readout** is the whole of what that
panel is. `wstat` is the first whisker program written for that rather than to
demonstrate a mechanism — the first one somebody would leave running.

**What to look at, in the order it is worth looking:**

1. **A tick on which nothing changed writes nothing at all.** No `setstat`, no
   bytes, no log line. That is the point of the program, and it is why the
   readings are at a resolution a person would choose — tenths of a degree,
   whole minutes — rather than the finest the source offers. Every band
   re-parses the whole document, so a panel repainting once a second for
   nothing is real time taken from a board that has other work, and it is
   invisible on a desk. Watching `wstat`'s stderr over ssh should show bursts
   and then silence, not a line a second.
2. **When something does change, only its rows are rewritten**, and the band
   carries the scene's background rather than the device's — the bug `flick`
   shipped for months. Several readings changing in one tick is one clipped
   write spanning them, not several, which is what the set form of
   `wsk_rows` is for.
3. **It degrades rather than lying.** `/tsens/0` is optional; if it is not
   there the row says `none` instead of showing a number that stopped being
   true.

**The stack is declared and NOT bisected**, and that is the one thing I cannot
do from here. `build.conf` says 8192 and shows its working: `-fstack-usage`
plus the call graph puts `wstat`'s deepest path to the write at **880 bytes**
against `wflick`'s **592**, and since `wflick` overflowed at 5120 and ran at
6144, most of that is your rasteriser rather than whisker. So `wstat`'s minimum
should sit near 6432 and 8192 is the kilobyte-plus margin `control` and `pic`
use. **Please bisect it** — I would rather it were measured than inferred, and
the inference leans on `R` being the same for two different documents, which
your own numbers say it is not.

**It builds against your vendored `modules/whisker.h` as committed** — pinned at
`d6b24c4`, double-linked, position independent, 5,228 bytes. It uses nothing
newer, so **no refresh is needed for this module**. whisker's header has moved
since (profiles, `WSK_E_KIND`) and `whisker.from` will say so when somebody
refreshes it for another reason.
